# Code of Conduct

We aim to make Ghost Bot a respectful and welcoming project. Participate in
good faith, give constructive feedback, respect differing experience and
backgrounds, and respect privacy and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, publication of private information
without consent, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer. Do not publish private incident
details in issues. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict
participation in proportion to the conduct. Reports should be handled with
discretion and without retaliation. For security vulnerabilities, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
